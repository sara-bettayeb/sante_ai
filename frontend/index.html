What is the Thyroid?

The thyroid is a butterfly-shaped gland located in the front of the neck. It produces hormones that help regulate metabolism, energy and body temperature.

What Does the Thyroid Do?

Metabolism

Helps regulate how the body uses energy.

Energy

Thyroid hormones influence energy levels.

Body Temperature

Helps regulate body temperature.

Thyroid Tests

TSH Test

Measures thyroid-stimulating hormone levels.

Free T4 Test

Measures the level of free thyroxine in the blood.

T3 Test

Measures triiodothyronine hormone levels.

Thyroid Antibodies

Helps identify autoimmune thyroid conditions.

These tests are used by healthcare professionals to evaluate thyroid function. Results should be interpreted by a healthcare professional.
